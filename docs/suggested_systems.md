# Suggested Systems

The domain suggests these major systems:

1. **Identity, contact, membership and authorization** — canonical human and machine identities, optional organizational identities usable by delegated real users, contacts, tenant creation and tenant admins, occasions and occasion admins, recruitment opening, teams, roles, permissions, and delegated administration.
2. **Participant registration and safeguarding** — occasion sign-up, health/allergy data, consent, scout-member imports, safe-from-harm evaluation, and guardian workflows.
3. **Volunteer/workforce management** — recruitment, interviews, onboarding, skills, scheduling, attendance, satisfaction, performance, and offboarding.
4. **CRM and relationship management** — participants, organizations, suppliers, families, teams, sponsors, and communication history.
5. **Ticketing and service management** — participant support, incidents, work orders, assignment, escalation, SLAs, and resolution tracking.
6. **Emergency and incident command** — accidents, fire, evacuation, perimeter security, major incidents, restricted handling, and response coordination.
7. **GIS and digital-twin camp management** — planning, camp design, infrastructure, live operational layers, closures, resources, and map-based dispatch.
8. **Closed-loop communications** — alarms, mass notifications, direct messages, team messaging, rich messaging, streaming, and conferencing.
9. **Environmental and food-safety monitoring** — water, food handling, air quality, sanitation, weather, sensors, inspections, thresholds, and compliance.
10. **Supply-chain and procurement management** — suppliers, purchasing, deliveries, replenishment, contracts, and demand forecasting.
11. **Inventory, asset and warehouse management** — stock, reservations, picking, issuing, returns, inspections, maintenance, losses, and invoicing.
12. **Closed-loop payments and occasion finance** — RFID accounts, readers, internal ledger, top-ups, refunds, limits, reconciliation, and settlement.
13. **Billing and accounting integration** — invoices, unpaid or damaged assets, supplier invoices, payroll-like settlements, financial reporting, and audit.
14. **Transport and traffic management** — access roads, parking, deliveries, shuttle services, congestion, route closures, and live traffic status.
15. **Facilities and utilities management** — tents, buildings, containers, toilets, water, power, network, waste, maintenance, and breakdown response.
16. **Security and access control** — perimeter guarding, credentials, restricted zones, lost wristbands, visitor control, and security incidents.
17. **Food-service and catering management** — menus, dietary requirements, suppliers, kitchens, food inventory, hygiene controls, and meal logistics.
18. **Scheduling and resource allocation** — shifts, venues, vehicles, equipment, teams, appointments, training, and operational capacity.
19. **Document, knowledge and procedure management** — runbooks, safety procedures, maps, forms, policies, training material, and versioned operational guidance.
20. **Operational intelligence and reporting** — real-time dashboards, KPIs, forecasts, capacity, response times, satisfaction, safety, financial, and post-occasion analysis.
21. **Data integration and occasion-platform services** — APIs, imports, audit trails, master data, notifications, search, workflow, offline operation, and synchronization between all systems.

The central architectural concept is likely a shared occasion platform with common identity, contact, membership, team, GIS location, ticket, communication, and audit services, surrounded by specialized operational modules.
