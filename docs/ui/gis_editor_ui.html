01 / Small, useful editing kit

Points for places and facilities. Lines for paths and utilities. Polygons for camp plots and activity zones. Select, move, reshape, snap, undo and measure. GeoJSON import/export with a preview and validation step.

No geoprocessing workbench or CAD toolchain.

02 / Open basemap, own camp layers

A lightweight Leaflet map can display OSM-derived tiles and editable GeoJSON overlays. Streets and land cover form the context; terrain contours or elevation need a separately sourced, licensed layer.

Provider, coverage, permitted caching and attribution configured per source.

03 / Draft, check, publish

Save geometry to a draft. Review changed features, overlaps and access before publishing a version. Preserve stable place IDs and history. Operational overlays remain read-only references to their owning systems.

Public OSM tile servers are not an offline-pack source; choose a suitable provider or self-hosted tiles for that need. Tile policy.
